# Lessons

> **What this is** : what the desk learned, beyond the strategy that taught it. Not "X failed", but what the failure (or the success) says about markets, about how to design a test, about data, about our own method.
> **Who writes** : the Analyst, the Red team and the Economist propose lines with their verdicts; the desk chief writes them here and merges duplicates.
> **Who reads** : the Ideator and the Economist, before proposing or judging an idea in the same family or asset. An idea a lesson rules out needs a reason why this time is different.

**Line format** : `YYYY-MM-DD · family / asset · lesson in one sentence · evidence: strategy, number · source: seat`
