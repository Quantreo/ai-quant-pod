**Code audit** (lines of `strategies/crypto_thin_losers.py`, against Pass 1, the Data check and the look-ahead patterns): no `shift(-k)`, no centred window, no full-sample normalisation. Signals use `cl.shift(14)` and a trailing 14-day rolling mean (lines 70-72); the quantiles are taken per row on that day's names (lines 86, 98-100); the beta regression window ends at row i inclusive and uses lags `hs[lo-k:i+1-k]` (lines 132-142), with the book's current weights on past returns, as Pass 1 writes; the vol scale uses the same window (151). The weight row t is traded at the close of t and earns from t+1 (lab/backtest docstring); my own reconstruction `pos.shift(1) * return` matches the engine price P&L (correlation 0.991 train, 0.992 validation). The universe row of t uses the volume of day t, which is complete at the close of t where the fill is. The gate regressor `uni.shift(1)` for the market return is the day before, and the market return of day t is concurrent with the book's return of day t, as a Dimson regression needs. No finding.
