### Round 1 · 2026-10-23 · Critic: revise

**Strongest rival explanation**: depth-of-fall reversal. Within the 30% bottom losers on 14-day return, a high Amihud (|R| over volume) mostly selects the deepest fallers, so "long thin losers, short liquid losers" is long the deepest losers and short the shallowest. Short-term reversal pays that spread with no liquidity payer, and it makes the winners spread negative (the deepest winners fall back), so P2 comes out as written under the rival. Only P5/V1 (volume-only sort) separates the two, and P5 is "read only".
