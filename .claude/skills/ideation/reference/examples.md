# Examples

From the Idea Journal, before the Ideator existed. Reuse the reasoning, not the topics.

## Good entries

- **Pre-ECB / pre-BoE version of the FOMC idea**: "Same hedging behaviour before other central banks, if the announcement is as scheduled and as watched as the Fed's." Transpose, and the condition under which the payer exists is stated.
- **Month-end USD flows at the London 4 pm fix**: "a flow with a known date, a known time, and a direction you can estimate from the month's equity move." Names who must trade, when, and in which direction.
- **Token unlocks in crypto**: a supply shock with a known date. First kill stated: "check first that the short side is tradable on the names concerned."

## Dropped, and why

- **Weekend effect in crypto**: "If it existed, the market makers who trade the weekend would have taken it; short sample, no reason behind it." No payer, and who would arbitrage it is obvious.
- **Buy the dip on BTC after a -10% day**: "'It always comes back' is not a mechanism: who is the forced seller, and why would he still be there tomorrow?"
