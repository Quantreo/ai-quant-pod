## abstract
The book holds three crypto strategies, Carry, Momentum and Low vol, and earns a net Sharpe ratio of 1.74 at 12.1% volatility from March 2021 to August 2026. The candidate, ETF TSMOM 12-Month, is a trend book on multi-asset ETFs that passed its sealed test, with a Sharpe ratio of 0.52 on this window. It is uncorrelated with every strategy of the book, while the three crypto strategies are not uncorrelated with each other. Added at entry weight it lifts the book to 1.82 and its diversification multiplier from 2.03 to 2.24; at full weight it barely helps. **The book improves in 77% of bootstrap histories at entry weight: the candidate goes on paper.** Two findings matter more than the candidate: the book's correlation to bitcoin, near zero for five years, reads 0.61 at the end of the window, and one suspect day, 30 April 2025, distorts six months of the book's risk statistics.

## book
Each strategy is scaled to 15% volatility with its trailing 60-day volatility, known the day before. The families share the risk equally and the book is brought to its 10% target by the diversification multiplier (IDM). The candidate enters at half its equal-risk weight, 12.5% of the risk, the three members shrinking from 33.3% to 29.2% each. Its Sharpe ratio of 0.52 is below Carry (0.99) and Momentum (1.20) but above Low vol (0.39).

## weights
The weight curve answers the first question: the book's Sharpe ratio rises with the candidate's weight up to 15% of the risk, where it reaches 1.82, then falls, because beyond that too much risk goes to a strategy with a third of the book's Sharpe ratio. At entry weight the book improves in 77% of histories, at full weight in 53%. Smaller weights are safer but add little: at 2.5% the book improves in 89% of histories, for a median gain of 0.021.

Low vol, the weakest member, looks like the one to cut, but it is not: without it the book falls to 1.35, and with the candidate in its place to 1.45, because Low vol moves against Carry and Momentum. The current weights stay; the candidate can only be an addition, at a small weight.

## performance
The gain at entry weight is small, from 1.74 to 1.82, and the bootstrap ranges overlap almost entirely (1.13 to 2.37 now, 1.20 to 2.44 with). Drawdown, worst month and tail loss do not change. By year, the candidate helped in 2021, 2022 and 2026 and cost 2.4 points in 2023.

## diversification
The members are not independent: Carry and Momentum move together (+0.30) and Low vol moves against both (-0.34 and -0.37), which is why it earns its place. The candidate is independent of all three, and its six-month correlation with the rest of the book stays between -0.25 and +0.21. The average pairwise correlation peaks at only +0.07, in April 2025.

The rolling view also shows what the full-window numbers hide. From April to October 2025 Low vol's correlation with the rest of the book drops to -0.84 and its share of risk swings to -48%: this is one day, 30 April 2025, when Carry gains 15.9%, Momentum 14.1% and Low vol loses 13.8%. It dominates six months of statistics and must be checked in the source data before the book is trusted. The candidate carries 6% of the book's risk on average and never more than 14%.

## exposures
The book is not a disguised market bet: its alpha is 18.5% a year with a t-statistic of 3.2, and the markets explain 5% of its variance. Carry has a negative beta to US equities (-0.27). The candidate, over these five years, was short long Treasuries (-0.23) and long gold (+0.17), both significant, and has no return left once the markets are accounted for (alpha -3.1%, t -0.8): its return here is the bond and gold trend of 2022 to 2025, which is what trend should earn, not an alpha.

The averages hide the swings. The book's correlation to US equities is +0.02 over the whole window but moves from +0.54 in September 2021 to -0.56 in October 2022, and is above 0.3 in absolute value in 45% of weeks. Its correlation to bitcoin, +0.01 over the window, reached +0.62 in August 2026 and reads +0.61 at the end. This is the reading to watch: if it persists, the book is no longer neutral to the market it trades.

## stress
The book made money in six of seven stress episodes, including the 2022 bear market in equities and bonds (+19.5%) and the Terra and FTX collapses. The candidate helped in 2022, when bonds and equities trended down (+6.0%), and cost in the two sharp reversals, the yen carry unwind of August 2024 (-1.3%) and the tariff shock of April 2025 (-1.4%). Trend is slow insurance: it pays in long bear markets, not in crashes of a few days.

## decision
The candidate passes the marginal rule after its luck haircut, since its correlation to the book is zero. The whole common window is after its train, so the decision rests on one share: at entry weight the book improves in 77% of histories. Under the routine, 60 to 90% goes on paper: it is tracked alongside the book without money and re-run each quarter; it enters at half weight if the share passes 90%. It stays in the library either way.

## needs
The book is well diversified inside crypto but has no second engine: almost all its return comes from one market, and its recent correlation to bitcoin says that market can pull it. It needs a premium on other markets with a Sharpe ratio near 1, carry or value on currencies or rates for instance, more than another uncorrelated strategy at 0.5.

## limits
The three members were built and selected on the years they are judged on here, so the book's 1.74 is in-sample and flatters it against any candidate; the comparison of weights is in-sample too. Their returns are imported without gross, so their correlations are measured after costs. The suspect day of 30 April 2025 is still in the data. Five and a half years and 66 months are short for the regressions: the betas are indicative.
