# Mechanism types

Classify the idea in one type. Each type has its questions: answer all of them, with labels. If no type fits, or the questions cannot be answered, the type is **unknown** and the verdict is Park.

## Risk premium
Someone pays you to hold a risk they want to shed.
- Which bad state of the world makes the strategy lose? When does it crash?
- Who sheds the risk, and why do they accept to pay?
- Is it a known factor in disguise (market, carry, short volatility, liquidity)?

## Behavioural bias
Some investors make a systematic mistake, and the strategy takes the other side.
- Which bias, and in whom?
- Why do arbitrageurs not erase it (cost, short constraints, risk, capacity)?
- Should it weaken once published? Since when is it public?

## Constraint
A participant must trade for a reason unrelated to expected return: mandate, leverage, regulation, index rules, tax.
- Which participant, which constraint?
- Is the flow observable or predictable, and in which direction?
- How large is the flow compared to the capacity of the strategy?

## Scheduled flow
A participant must trade at a known time: calendar, event, rebalancing, expiry, announcement.
- What sets the date, and who must trade around it?
- Why at that moment and not earlier or later?
- What does an ordinary day with the same window look like (placebo)?

## For every type
- **What it could secretly be**: market beta, carry, short volatility, liquidity provision, a size effect, a trend in the sample, a data artefact.
- **Predictions**: at least two, each one a result the Diagnostic can check. Name them P1, P2...
